Why we build on modern web tech, not page builders

Template builders are quick for the agency and expensive for you. Here's what you're actually buying when a site is built on one.

WBA 28 July 2026 · 1 min read · Build

The trade nobody explains

A page builder saves the agency days of work. That saving is real — but it gets spent once, by them, and the cost gets paid every day afterwards, by you: in load time, in what the site can never do, and in the rebuild you'll need in three years.

Speed is a revenue number

Most people reach your site on a phone, on a patchy signal, while doing something else. A heavy theme loading a dozen plugins loses a chunk of them before the page has drawn.

The short version

Build it lean once, and it can grow. Build it on a template, and the template decides your ceiling.
