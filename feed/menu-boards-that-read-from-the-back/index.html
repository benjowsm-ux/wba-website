Menu boards that read from the back of the queue

A café menu is a design problem with a very specific constraint: it has to be legible from eight metres away, in a hurry.

WBA 5 August 2026 · 1 min read · Create

The constraint

Nobody reads a menu board standing still. They read it from the back of a queue, at an angle, while deciding whether to stay. That single fact decides the type size, the contrast and the ordering.

What changed

Fewer items per panel, prices aligned on a single axis, and the two things the café actually wants to sell sitting where the eye lands first.
