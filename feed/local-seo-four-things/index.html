Local SEO: the four things that actually move the needle

Most local search advice is filler. For a business with one location, almost all of the result comes from four things.

WBA 18 August 2026 · 1 min read · Grow

1. Your Google Business profile

Complete, categorised correctly, with real photos and accurate hours. This is the single highest-return hour of work available to a local business.

2. Reviews, steadily

A trickle every month beats a burst once a year. Recency counts, and a dead review feed reads as a dead business.

3. Being consistent about your name and address

The same business name, address and phone number everywhere it appears. Mismatches are the most common quiet problem we find.

4. A page per thing you actually do

One page listing nine services will lose to nine pages that each do one properly.

Everything else

Is optimisation on top of these four. Do them first.
