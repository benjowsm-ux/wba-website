A brand is a set of rules, not a logo

The logo is the easy part. What makes a small business look established is everything that stays the same underneath it.

WBA 14 July 2026 · 1 min read · Create

Why consistency reads as size

People judge how established a business is by how consistent it looks — the same two typefaces, the same handful of colours, the same tone on the sign as on the invoice.

A brand you can hand to somebody else is worth more than one that only lives in the owner's head. Rules make it transferable.

The minimum useful brand

Two typefaces. Three colours. One logo in three formats. A single page saying how to use them. That's genuinely it.
